SATs Lab

The SaTS Lab is an interdisciplinary research lab focused on designing inclusive, accessible, and relational technologies. We work with people living with dementia, immigrant and racialized communities, people with speech or cognitive impairments, and other equity-deserving groups to co-create communication systems that prioritize dignity, agency, and care.


Dr. Shital Desai

Dr. Shital Desai is an Associate Professor of Interaction Design in the School of Arts, Media, Performance & Design and the York Research Chair in Accessible Interaction Design at York University. She directs the Social and Technological Systems (SaTS) Lab, where her community-engaged research focuses on designing accessible technologies for marginalized groups—including people with physical and cognitive impairments, older adults, children, and individuals on the autism spectrum.
Her work examines how technologies can support everyday activities, communication, and relational closeness by understanding people’s needs, habits, and rituals, and by adapting to these dynamic contexts of use.


Design for Health, Aging, and Cognitive Care

Our research program on Design for Health, Aging and Cognitive Care is structured around six interrelated strands that address communication, care, cultural relevance, rehabilitation, and inclusivity in health contexts.
